What I draw

Character illustrations, original character designs, stylized portraits, and expression sheets.

Each can be done as a sketch, line art, ink, or complete illustration, in digital or traditional.

Original character designs and complete (colored) illustrations are digital only. I do offer colored tradition pieces.

Traditional pieces are drawn on 8x10 sketch paper and shipped to you.

I also design logos and posters.

Examples

  • A character illustration is a full body drawing of your character (or an idea you have) in the pose of your choice, with a plain or simple background.

  • An original character design sheet includes 3 pose illustrations, 3 expressions, and a mini turn around view of the character.

  • A stylized portrait is a full body illustration of a real person (including yourself!) drawn in my style. Send photos for reference.

  • Expression sheets include 6 expressions of your choice, or my choice if you're feeling indecisive!

  • Line art is simply clean lines with no color, for all you minimalists out there.

  • Traditional ink pieces are drawn with real ink on paper. Digital ink illustrations imitate the look of traditional ink on screen for a lower price.

  • A custom logo for your brand, channel, or project. I'll send a few concept sketches to choose from, then refine your favorite into the final design. You'll receive high-resolution PNG files, with and without a transparent background.

  • Poster / Flyer designs are sent digitally only, I do not handle any printing.

Process

  1. Send an inquiry with the form below.
  2. I reply with a quote and a time estimate.
  3. You pay 50% upfront.
  4. I send a concept for your approval and make changes until you're happy with it.
  5. I finalize your piece.
  6. You pay the remaining 50%, and I send your finished piece (traditional pieces are shipped).

Terms

Read the terms

Payment. Prices are in US dollars. I accept PayPal and Cash App. 50% of the quoted price is paid upfront before I start. The remaining 50% is due before I send you the finished piece.

Revisions. Ask for as many changes as you'd like during the sketch and concept stage, that's what it's there for! Once we move into finalizing, I can make small changes within reason. After the piece reaches the final stage we agreed on, further revisions may no longer be possible.

Refunds. If you cancel before your commission reaches the finalization stage, your 50% deposit can be refunded. Once the piece is in finalization or beyond, refunds are no longer available.

Turnaround. Characters are my specialty. If you choose "Other" and ask for something I don't draw often, like a car or another object, it may take a good bit longer. Most pieces take about [2–4 weeks]. I'll give you a time estimate with your quote, and if you have a deadline, let me know in the form so I can tell you whether I can meet it.

Shipping. Traditional pieces are drawn on 8x10 sketch paper and shipped to you once the remaining payment is made. Shipping is added to your quote and sent with tracking. If you live outside the US, ask in your inquiry and I'll let you know whether I can ship to you. I pack every piece carefully, but once it's with the carrier I can't be responsible for loss or damage. I'll help you file a claim if anything goes wrong.

Digital delivery. Digital pieces are sent as high-resolution PNG files. Logos come with and without a transparent background.

What I don't draw. I'm open to most ideas, but I don't take NSFW commissions. I may also decline any request I'm not comfortable with.

Using your commission. Once your commission is paid for, you can use it personally: print it for yourself, use it as a profile picture, or share it online with credit to me. Commercial use, like selling prints or merch, needs my permission and may cost extra. Logos and posters are made for business use, so commercial use is included for those. I keep the copyright to all my artwork.

Sharing. In the form, you can choose whether I may show your commission in my gallery and portfolio or keep it private. I'll respect whichever you pick.
